1. Executive Summary

This blueprint defines the architecture, content hierarchy and technical optimization strategy for epitexsolution.africa. Epitex
Solutions acts as the regional distributor, system integrator and lifecycle maintainer in East Africa for Tier-1 defense, aerospace and
high-technology manufacturers. The regional footprint is anchored on STM (Savunma Teknolojileri Mühendislik ve Ticaret A..).

The website must position Epitex Solutions as the regional engineering backbone, not a commercial broker. It must show that
Epitex can deploy, maintain and support autonomous systems, naval technologies, cyber intelligence and mission software for
sovereign defense agencies and civil authorities.

5.2 Strategic takeaway for the web presence

The site must counter the perception that Epitex is only an intermediary. Content should repeatedly show local engineering
validation: test beds, factory-trained software engineers and field deployment units able to deliver STM capability to regional
procurement authorities. Each of these statements needs supporting evidence [V-5].

5.3 Trust and verification anchors
· Compliance badges: ICAO interoperability, Eurocontrol alignment, ISO status and verified STM partner seal (only where

    substantiated).
· A dedicated Procurement Compliance notice covering sovereign acquisition rules, dual-use export authorizations and secure

    communications protocols.
· A persistent domain switcher on every page, with the four operational domains listed in section 3.
